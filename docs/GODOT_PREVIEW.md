Preview of the Godot edition, still being ported from build-13. Not the finished game.

- In this build: the machines ported so far, on a temporary start screen that lists them. A machine
  costs a token, as in the hall.
- Not yet: the 3D hall, the title screen, the photo booth and the machines still being ported.
- Installs over build-13 and over 2.0.0 and keeps your tokens, tickets, high scores, prizes, photo
  strips and options. The old save files are left untouched.
- The finished release will install over this one.
