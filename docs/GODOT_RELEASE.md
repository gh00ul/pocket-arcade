Pocket Arcade, Godot edition: a faithful port of the Kotlin build-13 to Godot 4.6.2.

Installs over build-13 and over 2.0.0 and keeps progress and settings (tokens, tickets, prizes,
high scores, photo strips, options). arm64-v8a, signed with the same key as every earlier build.

See docs/PORT_PARITY.md in the repository for what was ported, what differs and why.
